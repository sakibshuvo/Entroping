# Code Of Conduct

Entroping uses a simple contributor standard: be direct, respectful, evidence-driven, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat contributors with respect.
- Critique code, docs, tests, and product decisions with concrete evidence.
- Assume good intent while still requiring verification.
- Keep issues and PRs focused on reproducible behavior, source evidence, or clearly stated proposals.
- Avoid harassment, insults, personal attacks, and discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation for behavior that makes the project unsafe or unproductive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. Security vulnerabilities should use `SECURITY.md` instead.
